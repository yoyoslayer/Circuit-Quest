## Open questions

- **Commit-then-reveal vs. visible blocks (R5-048):** the two verifier corrections conflict (teach: hide stop marks too; play: keep the qualitative path cue, hide only the numbers, and don't count unseen failures). I went with play's: the map and hall still show where the path stops or misses, and only measured setups show dB. Cycling bands therefore still shows which bands are *stopped* at an opaque wall, but never how strong a passing one is.
- **Mistake rule:** a failed send counts only when the failure was already visible (blocked/missed or a re-send of a measured setup). The 'No failed send' bonus is now about ignoring what you were shown, not about first tries.
- **Propped doors:** propping the lab or storeroom door lets broadcasts in through the doorway (truthful), and it is graded like any other setup (a propped lab door can make radio 1 mW an Elegant chaos shortcut). Only the quiet room caps an open door at Reliable. Is that the intended grading for chaos shortcuts?
- **Microwave through the open hatch** is now a reliable mirror-free route into the lab at 10 mW (verifier asked for it to be accepted deliberately). The job card doesn't mention it.
